# D0 deterministic selector assessment

Status: **NOT MEANINGFUL; NOT RUN**

The frozen T1 development requests expose several candidate causes and checks
with overlapping evidence support. A deterministic selector that chooses only
the first or most frequent candidate would not be an honest evidence-grounded
baseline: it would encode a position or frequency rule rather than resolve the
ambiguous evidence. The validation selection therefore compares the official
base and trained adapter directly, and D0 is recorded as not meaningful rather
than manufacturing a deterministic quality score.
